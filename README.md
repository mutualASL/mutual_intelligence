# Mutual - ASL Fingerspelling Interpreter and LLM Processor

Note: This is our work-in-progress repository that features our much older iterations of experimental models and datasets. The project began at the end of 2024 and has evolved into full development. 

## Progress Reflection: 
7/6/26: Our older iterations of classification models, model-training code, and program design have constructed the foundations for our current development. The initial iterations highlighted the need for hand motion tracking to mitigate environmental disturbances, and our early successes inspired the objective of building an interactive wearable interpreter for asl fingerspelling. After consulting with local Beijing workers who are hard-of-hearing and reevaluating our approach, our prior iterations have guided us to center our mission on inspiring students and the hearing community to proactively engage with the Deaf community by learning aspects of ASL.
